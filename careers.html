The role will involve working closely with hardware, firmware, mechanical, signal-integrity, product, and manufacturing teams to develop reliable and production-ready designs. The ideal candidate should have strong hands-on experience in multilayer PCB design, high speed interfaces, power electronics, mixed-signal circuits, and DFM/DFT, with the ability to understand a complete system rather than focusing only on PCB layout.

1. Schematic Design

  • Develop complete schematics for embedded, industrial, automotive, networking, and other electronic systems.
  • Translate system requirements and block diagrams into detailed schematics.
  • Review datasheets, application notes, reference designs, and design guidelines.
  • Define power architecture, clocking, reset, programming/debug, communication, and protection circuits.
  • Perform schematic reviews for functionality, connectivity, signal integrity, power integrity, and manufacturability.
  • Maintain schematic libraries, symbols, footprints, and component attributes.
  • Generate and maintain BOMs and engineering documentation.

  • Perform complete multilayer PCB layout, including:
    • Component placement
    • Power and ground planes
    • Signal routing
    • Differential-pair routing
    • Length/skew matching
    • Controlled-impedance routing
    • Via and layer-transition optimization
  • Develop PCB stack-ups based on electrical, mechanical, and manufacturing requirements.
  • Implement appropriate signal-return-path and grounding strategies.
  • Optimize layouts for EMI/EMC performance.
  • Work with high-density packages including BGA, QFN, QFP, and fine-pitch components.
  • Perform PCB design-rule checks and resolve layout violations.
  • Generate fabrication and assembly outputs including Gerber/ODB++, drill files, pick-and-place, assembly drawings, and manufacturing documentation.

3. High-Speed Design

  • Hands-on experience with high-speed interfaces such as:
    • Ethernet / Gigabit Ethernet
    • USB 2.0 / USB 3.x
    • PCIe
    • DDR / LPDDR
    • MIPI
    • RGMII / RMII / SGMII
    • CAN / CAN-FD
    • SPI / I²C / UART

4. Power & Mixed-Signal PCB Design

  • PCB design experience with DC/DC converters, LDOs, PMICs, power stages, and battery powered systems.
  • High-current trace and power-plane design.
  • Understanding of switching regulator layout requirements.
  • Analog/digital partitioning.
  • Low-noise analog layout.
  • ADC/DAC and sensor interface layout.
  • Grounding and power-distribution strategy.
  • Thermal considerations for high-power components.

5. Design for Manufacturing

  • Work closely with PCB fabrication and assembly vendors.
  • Apply DFM, DFA, DFT, and manufacturing guidelines during design.
  • Understand PCB fabrication processes, tolerances, via structures, solder-mask requirements, and assembly constraints.
  • Review fabrication and assembly feedback and implement necessary changes.

Job Description

Location: California (Carlsbad), USA

Experience: 3–15 years

Position Summary

We are seeking two highly motivated, hands-on Server Qualification / Validation Engineers to support server qualification activities at our Carlsbad engineering site. The ideal candidates will have strong experience in server hardware, system-level testing, platform validation, and debugging. They will work closely with engineering teams to execute qualification plans, identify issues, troubleshoot failures, and ensure server platforms meet quality, reliability, and performance requirements.

Key Responsibilities

  • Execute server hardware and system-level qualification test plans.
  • Perform functional, stress, performance, stability, and reliability testing on server platforms.
  • Set up, configure, and maintain server test environments and lab infrastructure.
  • Validate server subsystems, including CPU, memory, storage, PCIe, networking, and power/thermal components.
  • Perform BIOS/UEFI, BMC, and firmware validation and regression testing.
  • Investigate system failures, analyze logs, reproduce issues, and assist with root-cause analysis.
  • Execute automated and manual test suites, document results, and track defects through closure.
  • Work closely with hardware, firmware, software, and system engineering teams.
  • Provide regular qualification status reports, test coverage updates, and issue summaries.
  • Support server bring-up, integration, and qualification milestones.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.
  • 3+ years of hands-on experience in server validation, system qualification, hardware testing, or platform engineering.
  • Strong understanding of x86 server architecture and server subsystems.
  • Hands-on experience with Linux, including command-line tools, system logs, and troubleshooting.
  • Experience debugging hardware, firmware, and system-level failures.
  • Familiarity with BIOS/UEFI, BMC/IPMI/Redfish, PCIe, DDR memory, NVMe/SSD, and Ethernet.
  • Experience executing qualification test plans and documenting results.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced engineering lab environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Intel Xeon or AMD EPYC server platforms.
  • Experience with Python, Bash, or other scripting languages for test automation.
  • Familiarity with server management and diagnostic tools.
  • Experience with stress and performance tools such as stress-ng, fio, iperf3, and memtester.
  • Experience with server OEM/ODM qualification processes.
  • Familiarity with issue tracking systems such as Jira.
  • Experience with rack-level testing, server integration, and hardware failure analysis.

Ideal Candidate

A hands-on engineer who is comfortable working directly with server hardware, running qualification tests, debugging failures, analyzing results, and collaborating with cross-functional engineering teams to meet aggressive project schedules.

Qualification:

  • Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.
